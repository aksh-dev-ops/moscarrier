FAQ

Common questions

How does the career assessment work?

It's an online questionnaire that looks at your interests, strengths, personality and values. The results feed into a report that suggests careers that suit you.

Are sessions online or in person?

Most sessions are held over video so you can join from anywhere. In-person sessions may be available on request.

Can parents join the sessions?

Yes. For school students we encourage parents to attend at least one session.

Is this useful if I'm already working?

Yes. We help professionals plan career changes, find skill gaps and set long-term goals.
